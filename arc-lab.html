How the game works

Take five free throws from the same spot. Choose a launch angle and speed, then watch the flight. A basket counts when the ball’s center crosses the hoop opening on the way down. Compare consecutive trails to decide how to adjust your next shot.

Learning outcomes

After playing, you should be able to

  1. Predict how increasing launch speed changes the ball’s horizontal travel distance when the launch angle stays the same.
  2. Use two shots at the same speed to explain how a larger launch angle changes the ball’s peak height.

The model uses constant gravity (9.81 m/s²) and ignores air resistance and rebounds. The reported range is the distance to the floor if nothing interrupts the shot.
